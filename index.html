      My Contributions to Published Papers

      CXRG-SVLM: Chest X-ray Report Generation

      Scientific Reports · 2026

      My contribution: I collaborated on the small vision-language model and helped implement 4-bit QLoRA for efficient fine-tuning.

      What the study found: The team combined frontal and lateral chest X-rays with clinical indication text to generate the Findings section of reports. On the Indiana University dataset, the model scored 0.3527 ROUGE-L and 0.2744 METEOR, with 1.91% of its loaded parameters trainable and an average generation time of 5.27 seconds per report on one GPU. The paper calls for follow-up evaluation by radiologists.

      Read the CXRG-SVLM abstract

      Ethics of Large Language Models in Healthcare

      Frontiers in Digital Health · 2025

      My contribution: I contributed to formal analysis and helped review and edit the manuscript.

      What the study found: The team reviewed 27 papers and identified bias, patient safety, transparency, accountability, and privacy as recurring concerns. The review proposed an initial framework for responsible use of large language models in healthcare.
